Custom Measurement Service

Photo to Pattern

Upload a garment reference image and provide the intended fit, fabric and construction details. We review the information and prepare professional measurement guidance to support pattern development and fit evaluation.

How This Service Works

From Reference Image to Better Measurement Decisions

This service is designed for brand owners and sampling teams who have a visual garment reference but need technical measurement guidance before developing or correcting the pattern.

Step 01

Upload the Design

Provide a clear garment reference image so the intended style and construction can be understood.

Step 02

Explain the Fit

Tell us the garment category, fabric, intended fit and important details such as pockets, ribbing or special cuts.

Step 03

Technical Review

Your submitted details are reviewed to prepare professional measurement guidance for fit and pattern development.

Step 04

Use It for Sampling

Use the guidance with your pattern master or CAD team to develop, review or correct the style before production.

What you receive: measurement guidance based on the submitted image and technical details. This is a professional development service, not an automatic AI-generated production pattern. Complex or highly specialized garments may require a separate custom review or quote.
