Why These Measurement Sheets Matter

Reliable References for Better Pattern Development

Good garment development starts with reliable body measurements. These reference sheets provide standardized dummy/body measurements across Men, Women and Kids categories, giving clothing brands, pattern makers and sampling teams a consistent technical starting point.

01

Start With a Reliable Base

Instead of estimating body measurements every time you develop a new style, use a consistent category- and size-based reference as your starting point.

02

Useful for New Clothing Brands

If you do not yet have your own established measurement standards, these sheets can provide a practical foundation for pattern development and sampling.

03

Check Pattern & Fit Proportions

Pattern makers and masters can compare important body measurements while developing or reviewing patterns and identifying possible proportion or fit issues.

04

Develop Your Own Garment Spec

Use the body measurements as a reference, then apply the required ease according to the garment type and intended fit — such as slim, regular, relaxed or oversized.

05

Maintain Sizing Consistency

Working from the same measurement foundation can help maintain more consistent sizing as your product range and collection expand.

Important: These are body/dummy measurement references, not finished garment measurements. Garment ease and design allowance must be added according to the garment type and intended fit. Final garment measurements will vary according to design, fabric, construction, intended fit and required ease.
